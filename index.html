Free Online Image Compressor

Compressly is a simple online image compression tool designed to reduce image file sizes while keeping the image quality as good as possible.

Upload your JPG, PNG or WebP image, select your preferred compression quality and download the optimized image.

For privacy, the compression process happens locally in your browser rather than uploading the image to a remote server.
